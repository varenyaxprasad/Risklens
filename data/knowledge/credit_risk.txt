Credit Risk Fundamentals

Credit risk is the possibility that a borrower may fail to repay
a loan according to the agreed terms.

Factors that may influence credit risk include credit score,
income, existing debt obligations, employment stability,
loan amount, interest rate, and repayment period.

A credit score is one indicator of creditworthiness. Lenders
should consider multiple factors rather than relying on a single
indicator when assessing a loan application.

The debt-to-income ratio compares a borrower's monthly debt
payments with their monthly income. A higher ratio may indicate
less capacity to take on additional debt.
